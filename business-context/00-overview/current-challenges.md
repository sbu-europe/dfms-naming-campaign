# Current-State Challenges & Business Impact

> Problems organizations face and the impact of these challenges

## Current-State Challenges (per PRD)

Organizations currently face:

- **Manual PR / TR / TO Handling**: Fragmented manual processes across departments
- **No Real-Time Visibility**: No visibility into vehicle location or status
- **Ad-hoc Vendor Selection**: Inconsistent vendor selection and rate management
- **Disconnected QC Workflows**: QC and lab workflows not integrated
- **Limited Capacity Awareness**: Limited stock and capacity awareness at POIs
- **Manual Costing**: Manual costing, invoicing, and reconciliation
- **High Dependency on Human Coordination**: Reliance on tribal knowledge and manual coordination

## Business Impact (per PRD)

These challenges result in:

- **Delivery Delays**: Missed commitments and delayed deliveries
- **Vehicle Idle Time**: Congestion at POIs and inefficient resource utilization
- **Quality Disputes**: No evidence trails for quality issues
- **Cost Leakage**: Poor vendor and trip costing visibility
- **Compliance Risks**: Audit and regulatory compliance challenges

## Solution Impact

DFMS addresses these challenges by providing a single operational backbone covering the complete lifecycle from Product Requisition → Transport → Delivery → QC → Costing, enabling:

- **Centralized, integrated platform** - Single source of truth
- **Automated key logistics workflows** - Reduces manual effort
- **Real-time visibility and tracking** - Live GPS monitoring
- **Data accuracy and operational transparency** - Complete audit trail
- **Improved resource utilization and cost optimization** - Data-driven decisions
