## 🎯 Key Insights Summary

### Top Pain Points
1. **Manual Processes** - Heavy reliance on Excel and paper-based tracking
2. **Lack of Real-time Visibility** - No live tracking of vehicles and shipments
3. **Communication Gaps** - Information silos between departments
4. **Data Accuracy** - Manual data entry leading to errors
5. **Vendor Management** - Difficulty in tracking vendor performance

### Top Expectations
1. **Digital Transformation** - Move from manual to automated processes
2. **Real-time Tracking** - GPS-based vehicle and shipment tracking
3. **Centralized System** - Single source of truth for all operations
4. **Mobile Access** - Field teams need mobile applications
5. **Analytics & Reporting** - Data-driven decision making
