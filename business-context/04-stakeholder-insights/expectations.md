# Stakeholder Expectations & Success Criteria

> What stakeholders expect from DFMS and how success will be measured

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary

Stakeholders across 7 departments have articulated clear expectations for DFMS. The overarching theme is **digital transformation** - moving from manual, paper-based processes to an integrated, real-time, mobile-enabled platform.

**Top 3 Expectations**:
1. **Real-time Visibility** (100% of stakeholders)
2. **Mobile Access** (85% of stakeholders)
3. **Automated Workflows** (80% of stakeholders)

**Expected ROI**: 70% productivity improvement, 50% cost reduction, 90% data accuracy

---

## 🚫 What Stakeholders DON'T Want

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid

1. **Complex UI**
   - "Don't make it complicated like SAP"
   - Expectation: Simple, intuitive interface

2. **Slow Performance**
   - "Don't make us wait for pages to load"
   - Expectation: <2 second response time

3. **Rigid Workflows**
   - "Don't force us into a fixed process"
   - Expectation: Configurable workflows

4. **Poor Mobile Experience**
   - "Don't just shrink the web app"
   - Expectation: Native mobile experience

5. **Data Silos**
   - "Don't create another separate system"
   - Expectation: Integrated with existing systems

6. **Excessive Training Requirements**
   - "Don't require 1 week of training"
   - Expectation: Intuitive, minimal training needed
