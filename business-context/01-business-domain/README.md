# Business Domain

> Comprehensive business domain knowledge including industry context, business rules, and processes

## 📋 Purpose

This folder contains detailed business domain knowledge organized into three main areas:

1. **Industry Context** - Market landscape, trends, competitive analysis, and regulations
2. **Business Rules** - Business logic, constraints, and validation rules
3. **Business Processes** - End-to-end operational workflows
