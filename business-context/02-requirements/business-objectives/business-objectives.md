# DFMS Business Objectives - Overview

> Strategic business goals and success criteria for the Distribution and Fleet Management System

## Executive Summary

DFMS aims to transform distribution and fleet operations from manual, fragmented processes into a centralized, automated, and transparent digital platform. The system addresses critical operational inefficiencies while enabling data-driven decision-making, cost optimization, and scalable growth.

## 📋 Contents

This document serves as an overview and index for detailed business objectives documentation:

### Core Objectives

- **[Primary Business Objectives](./primary-objectives.md)** - 8 core strategic objectives:
  1. Centralize Fleet Management Operations
  2. Automate Transport Planning and Execution
  3. Enable Real-Time Fleet Visibility and Tracking
  4. Improve Vendor Collaboration and Transparency
  5. Ensure Quality Control and Compliance
  6. Optimize Cost Management and Financial Accuracy
  7. Enhance Operational Efficiency and Resource Utilization
  8. Enable Data-Driven Decision Making
