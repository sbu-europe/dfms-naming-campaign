Naming guidelines

What makes a qualified name

Test every idea against these four before you send it.

Relevant

Hints at distribution, fleets, routes, logistics or supply chain, without being a generic industry word.

Does it suggest movement or delivery?

Unique

Not close in sound or spelling to an existing brand, especially in logistics or software.

Did a quick search show nothing similar?

Memorable

Short, easy to say and spell after hearing it once, in any language.

Could a colleague type it from a phone call?

Brand-ready

Works on an app icon, an invoice and a trade-show banner, in any country.

Would a global enterprise trust it?

Must-haves

  • The .com domain is unregistered.
  • It is not listed for resale or as a premium domain.
  • One word, or at most two words combined.
  • No client names, and nothing tied to one country.
How to check a .com

Search it at any domain registrar

If it shows as available at the normal price, it qualifies. If it says "premium", "make an offer" or "for sale", someone owns it and wants to resell it. Skip that one.
