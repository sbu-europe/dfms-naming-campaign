# Vendor Mobile — Tenant Membership (Join & Approval)

## Tenant join approval (after platform registration)

- Vendor can view all **active tenants**.
- Vendor can request to join one or more tenants.
- A **Tenant Admin** must approve the join request. On approval, the admin **sets vendor type** (**Contractual** vs **Non-Contractual**) for that vendor **within that tenant** — the vendor does not choose type at registration or in the join request.
- Until approved (and type assigned), the vendor cannot:
  - View bid opportunities for that tenant
  - View assigned TOs and trips for that tenant
  - Submit bids for that tenant

## Per-tenant vendor type (after approval)

- **Contractual:** vendor may receive direct TO assignment for that tenant (no bidding for that relationship).
- **Non-Contractual:** vendor may see published bids and submit bids for that tenant.
