      The product

      The Engine Behind Every Delivery

      One operational backbone for companies that move goods at scale, from the first request to the final invoice.

      In short: DFMS helps companies move goods. It plans each delivery, finds a free truck, tracks it on the road, checks quality on arrival and shows what the trip cost, all in one system instead of spreadsheets, chat groups and phone calls.

      DFMS is a cloud platform that plans, runs, monitors and optimises transport, fleet and distribution across factories, feed mills, depots, warehouses and ports.

      Today many of these companies run on Excel sheets, WhatsApp groups, paper delivery slips and disconnected systems. DFMS replaces them with a single source of operational truth for product movement, fleet use, vendors, quality, cost and compliance.

      Naming guidelines

      What Makes A Qualified Name

      Test every idea against these four before you send it.

      Relevant

      Hints at distribution, fleets, routes, logistics or supply chain, without being a generic industry word.

      Does it suggest movement or delivery?

      Unique

      Not close in sound or spelling to an existing brand, especially in logistics or software.

      Did a quick search show nothing similar?

      Memorable

      Short, easy to say and spell after hearing it once, in any language.

      Could a colleague type it from a phone call?

      Brand-Ready

      Works on an app icon, an invoice and a trade-show banner, in any country.

      Would a global enterprise trust it?

      Must-Haves

      • The .com domain is unregistered.
      • It is not listed for resale or as a premium domain.
      • One word, or at most two words combined.
      • No client names, and nothing tied to one country.
      • No Bangla or other local-language names. It must work for customers worldwide.
