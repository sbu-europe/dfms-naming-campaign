## 🔄 Daily Workflow

### Task 1: Review Trip Costs
**Duration**: 30-60 minutes  
**Frequency**: Daily

**Steps**:
1. Navigate to "Trip Costs" → "Pending Review"
2. Review cost calculations
3. Verify cost components
4. Approve or reject costs
5. Adjust if needed

**Review Criteria**:
- Cost components reasonable
- Distance matches actual
- Weight matches records
- Additional charges justified
- Total within budget

---

### Task 2: Approve Invoices
**Duration**: 30-60 minutes  
**Frequency**: Daily

**Steps**:
1. Navigate to "Invoices" → "Pending Approval"
2. Review invoice details
3. Verify trip completion
4. Check vendor account
5. Approve or reject

**Invoice Review**:
- Invoice amount matches trip cost
- Vendor details correct
- Trip completed successfully
- Payment terms acceptable

---

### Task 3: Monitor ERP Sync
**Duration**: 15-30 minutes  
**Frequency**: Daily

**Steps**:
1. Navigate to "ERP Sync Status"
2. Review sync status
3. Check for failures
4. Retry failed syncs
5. Verify successful syncs

**Sync Monitoring**:
- Success rate
- Failed syncs
- Retry attempts
- Manual sync option

---

### Task 4: Process Payments
**Duration**: 30-60 minutes  
**Frequency**: Weekly

**Steps**:
1. Navigate to "Payments" → "Pending"
2. Review payment requests
3. Process payments
4. Update payment status
5. Record payment details

**Payment Methods**:
- Bank transfer
- Online payment
- Check payment
- Cash payment
