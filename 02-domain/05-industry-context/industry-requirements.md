# DFMS Industry-Specific Requirements

## Industry-Specific Requirements

### 6.1 Food & Beverage (Poultry, Dairy, etc.)

**Critical Requirements**:
- **Quality Control**: Mandatory QC at loading and unloading
- **Temperature Monitoring**: Cold chain compliance (future)
- **Hygiene Standards**: Cleaning and sanitation tracking
- **Traceability**: Batch tracking from source to destination
- **Shelf Life Management**: FIFO/FEFO compliance
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Food safety regulations

**DFMS Support**:
- ✅ Comprehensive QC workflows
- ✅ Photo/video evidence capture
- ✅ Sample tracking with QR codes
- ✅ Lab test management
- ⏳ Temperature monitoring (future)
- ⏳ Batch tracking (future)

---

### 6.2 FMCG Distribution

**Critical Requirements**:
- **Multi-Tier Distribution**: Depot → Distributor → Retailer
- **High Volume**: Large number of daily trips
- **SKU Complexity**: Hundreds of products
- **Promotions & Schemes**: Dynamic pricing
- **Return Management**: Reverse logistics

**DFMS Support**:
- ✅ Multi-POI support
- ✅ High transaction volume capability
- ✅ Product master management
- ⏳ Pricing management (future)
- ⏳ Return management (future)

---

### 6.3 Pharmaceuticals

**Critical Requirements**:
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Drug licensing, storage conditions
- **Cold Chain**: Temperature-controlled transport
- **Batch & Expiry Tracking**: Strict traceability
- **Security**: High-value cargo protection
- **Audit Trail**: Complete documentation

**DFMS Support**:
- ✅ Comprehensive audit logging
- ✅ QC workflows
- ✅ Document management
- ⏳ Temperature monitoring (future)
- ⏳ Batch tracking (future)

---

### 6.4 Manufacturing & Industrial

**Critical Requirements**:
- **Raw Material Procurement**: Inbound logistics
- **Inter-Plant Transfers**: Multiple manufacturing locations
- **Port Clearance**: Import/export handling
- **Heavy Cargo**: Specialized vehicles
- **JIT Delivery**: Time-critical deliveries

**DFMS Support**:
- ✅ Port consignment management
- ✅ Inter-plant transfer workflows
- ✅ C&F clearance tracking
- ✅ Vehicle type management
- ✅ Time-based scheduling
