# DFMS Success Factors

## Success Factors

### 7.1 Product Success Factors

1. **Comprehensive**: End-to-end solution (not point solution)
2. **User-Friendly**: Intuitive for low-tech-literacy users
3. **Mobile-First**: Field operations on mobile
4. **Localized**: Bangladesh-specific (language, regulations, payment)
5. **Affordable**: Competitive SaaS pricing
6. **Reliable**: 99.5%+ uptime
7. **Scalable**: Handles growth without performance degradation
8. **Flexible**: Configurable for different industries

---

### 7.2 Go-to-Market Success Factors

1. **Reference Client**: Nourish success story and testimonials
2. **Industry Focus**: Start with F&B, expand to FMCG, Pharma
3. **Local Presence**: Bangladesh-based sales and support
4. **Partnership**: GPS providers, ERP vendors, industry associations
5. **Pricing Strategy**: Competitive, value-based pricing
6. **Implementation Support**: Quick onboarding, comprehensive training
7. **Customer Success**: Proactive support, regular engagement

---

### 7.3 Adoption Success Factors

1. **Executive Sponsorship**: Top management buy-in
2. **Change Management**: Structured rollout, champions
3. **Training**: Comprehensive, role-based training
4. **Quick Wins**: Demonstrate value early
5. **User Feedback**: Continuous improvement based on feedback
6. **Support**: Responsive support during transition
