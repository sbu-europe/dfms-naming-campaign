# DFMS Industry Context

> Industry landscape, market trends, and domain knowledge for distribution and fleet management

## Executive Summary

The Distribution and Fleet Management System (DFMS) operates in the logistics and supply chain management industry, specifically targeting businesses with complex distribution networks and fleet operations. The primary market focus is Bangladesh, with potential expansion to similar emerging markets in South Asia and Southeast Asia.

## Quick Reference

### Key Industry Segments

1. **Food & Beverage Distribution** - Primary focus (Nourish Poultry)
2. **FMCG Distribution** - Large market opportunity
3. **Pharmaceuticals** - Regulatory complexity
4. **Manufacturing & Industrial** - Inter-plant transfers
5. **Third-Party Logistics (3PL)** - Different business model

### Primary Market: Bangladesh

- **TAM**: 5,000+ companies with fleet operations
- **SAM**: 1,000+ mid-to-large enterprises
- **SOM**: 100+ companies (Year 1-3 target)

### Key Challenges Addressed

- Manual processes and poor visibility
- Inefficient resource utilization
- Vendor management issues
- Quality control gaps
- Financial and compliance challenges
