# DFMS Market Entry Strategy

## Market Entry Strategy

### 10.1 Phase 1: Establish in Bangladesh (Year 1-2)

**Target**: 20-30 clients
**Focus**: Food & Beverage, FMCG
**Strategy**:
- Leverage Nourish reference
- Industry-specific marketing
- Partnership with GPS providers
- Local sales team

---

### 10.2 Phase 2: Expand in Bangladesh (Year 2-3)

**Target**: 50-100 clients
**Focus**: Add Pharmaceuticals, Manufacturing
**Strategy**:
- Case studies and testimonials
- Industry events and conferences
- Channel partnerships
- Feature expansion based on feedback

---

### 10.3 Phase 3: Regional Expansion (Year 3-5)

**Target**: 200+ clients across South Asia
**Focus**: India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka
**Strategy**:
- Localization for each market
- Local partnerships
- Regional sales teams
- Market-specific features
