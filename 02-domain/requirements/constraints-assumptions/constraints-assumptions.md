## ⚠️ Critical Constraints

1. **Timeline**: Phase 1 (MVP) must be delivered within 26 weeks
2. **Scope**: Phase 1 scope is fixed (63 user stories)
3. **Multi-tenancy**: Must support multi-tenancy from day one
4. **Integration**: Must integrate with various ERPs and GPS providers
5. **Data Residency**: Some clients may require data to remain in Bangladesh
